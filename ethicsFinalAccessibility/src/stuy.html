Boys Junior Varsity Baseball

The Boys Junior Varsity Baseball team will be holding an interest meeting on Thursday, January 15th in Room 513.  Anyone interested should attend. No experience necessary. Please email Coach Meehan at RMeehan2@schools.nyc.gov

Girls Varsity Badminton

The Girls Varsity Badminton team will be holding an interest meeting on Thursday.  Come to learn about our season and tryouts or to hear about the Stuy Birdies badminton club. The meeting is January 15 after 10th period in Room 635.
